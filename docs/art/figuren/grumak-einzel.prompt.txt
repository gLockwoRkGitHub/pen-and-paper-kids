One friendly middle-aged orc spice merchant standing alone on plain cream watercolor paper, full body,
three-quarter front view, centered, soft ground shadow. He is huge, as tall and broad as a wardrobe,
with heavy shoulders and big gentle hands. Grey-green skin, two small lower tusks, a broad kind face
with tired, worried eyes and a warm half-smile. Long black hair with grey streaks tied back,
small gold rings in both pointed ears. He wears a loose rust-red merchant tunic with gold trim,
a wide brown leather belt hung with small spice pouches, a saffron-yellow sash, dark baggy trousers
and sturdy sandals. He holds a small scoop of roasted nuts in one hand.
Fantasy harbor-town setting, not scary, not a warrior, no weapons, no armor.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines,
visible paper texture and soft washes, warm golden light, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book
for young readers. Plain background, no scenery. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers.
