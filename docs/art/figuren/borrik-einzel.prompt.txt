One ancient blind dwarf stonemason standing alone on plain cream watercolor paper, full body, three-quarter front view, centered, soft ground shadow. He is very old, short and broad like all dwarves, with a bald head, deep wrinkles and a gentle patient smile. His eyes are blind: milky white with no pupils. His snow-white beard is so long that its end is tucked into his belt. He wears a thick grey stonemason apron covered in pale stone dust over a simple earth-brown tunic, heavy boots, and rests one hand on a short walking staff carved from stone. His other hand is slightly raised, feeling the air. Fantasy setting, no modern clothing, not scary, no weapons.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture and soft washes, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book for young readers. Plain background, no scenery. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers.
