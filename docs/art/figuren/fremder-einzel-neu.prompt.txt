# Zeichen des Kreises der Vier = vier verschlungene Ringe (docs/art/requisiten/ringzeichen.png). Prüfen: keine Drehform/kein Windrad, keine Audi-Reihe, keine olympische Anordnung.
One tall, slender mysterious traveller standing alone on plain cream watercolor paper, full body, three-quarter front view, centered, soft ground shadow. Taller than a normal man, upright calm posture, age impossible to guess. He wears a long, worn grey travelling cloak, open at the front over simple grey-brown travel clothes and a belt, clearly a wanderer's cloak and not a monk's robe, with a wide hood; the hem is patched in a few places. The cloak is fastened at the throat with a small round bronze clasp shaped like four small interlinked rings. His face lies in the deep shadow of the hood, but not completely black: a pale narrow chin and a mouth with a faint quiet smile are dimly visible; the upper face is hidden in shadow. His eyes are silvery and softly shimmering like moonlight reflected on water, reflecting light, not glowing like lamps. Slender hands in worn grey leather gloves. One hand holds a pale, smooth, polished driftwood walking staff as tall as his shoulder; the other hand is empty. Sturdy brown leather boots covered in pale grey mountain dust. Quiet, kind and enigmatic, not threatening, no weapons, no letter. Fantasy setting, no modern clothing.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture and soft washes, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book for young readers. Plain background, no scenery. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers. Cool grey-blue tones.
