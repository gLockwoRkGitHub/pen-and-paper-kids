# Das Gesicht bleibt im Schatten der Kapuze: Wer oder was der Fremde ist, bleibt offen.
# Zeichen des Kreises der Vier = vier verschlungene Ringe (docs/art/requisiten/ringzeichen.png). Prüfen: keine Drehform/kein Windrad, keine Audi-Reihe, keine olympische Anordnung.
One tall mysterious stranger standing alone on plain cream watercolor paper, full body,
three-quarter front view, centered, soft ground shadow. The figure wears a long plain grey travelling cloak
that reaches the ankles, with a deep hood pulled low so the face is completely hidden in shadow.
Inside the dark hood only two eyes are visible, glowing softly pale silver-white like moonlight on water.
Worn leather boots covered in pale grey mountain dust. One gloved hand holds a folded letter closed with
a dark wax seal stamped with four small interlinked rings. Calm, still and quiet posture; mysterious and a little eerie,
but not threatening, no weapons.
Fantasy setting, no modern clothing.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines,
visible paper texture and soft washes, cool grey-blue tones, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book
for young readers. Plain background, no scenery. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers.
