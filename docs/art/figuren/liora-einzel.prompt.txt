# Liora, Einzelbild im Normalzustand (Vorschlag von Claude – Haarfarbe und Kleidungsfarben sind noch nicht festgelegt)
# Riss im Ärmel und Blätter im Haar gehören nur in die Sturz-Szene, nicht hierher.
One eleven-year-old girl standing alone on plain cream watercolor paper, full body, three-quarter front view,
centered, soft ground shadow. Tall and lanky for her age, preteen proportions, not a small child.
She is the adventurous daughter of a very rich merchant family, well-groomed and expensively dressed:
shoulder-length chestnut-brown curly hair tied back with a dark green silk ribbon, a few curls escaping,
freckles, bright hazel eyes, a broad cheeky grin. She wears a spotless, perfectly tailored
deep-green velvet jacket with small gold buttons and fine gold embroidery on the cuffs,
a crisp white linen shirt with a neat collar, clean dark brown knee breeches,
white stockings, and polished brown leather shoes with silver buckles. Clothes clean and undamaged.
Hands on her hips, confident and friendly, a spark of mischief.
Fantasy harbor-town setting, no modern clothing.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines,
visible paper texture and soft washes, warm golden light, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book
for young readers. Plain background, no scenery. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers.
