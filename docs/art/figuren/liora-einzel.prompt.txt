# Liora, Einzelbild (Vorschlag von Claude – Haarfarbe und Kleidungsfarben sind noch nicht festgelegt)
One eleven-year-old girl standing alone on plain cream watercolor paper, full body, three-quarter front view,
centered, soft ground shadow. She is the adventurous daughter of a very rich merchant family
who has just fallen out of an apple tree: wild shoulder-length chestnut-brown curly hair with a few green
leaves stuck in it, freckles, bright hazel eyes, a broad cheeky grin. She wears an expensive
deep-green velvet jacket with small gold buttons, the left sleeve torn open at the shoulder,
a white linen shirt with a crumpled collar, dark brown knee breeches with grass stains on the knees,
white stockings, and fine brown leather shoes with silver buckles. Hands on her hips, confident and friendly.
Fantasy harbor-town setting, no modern clothing.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines,
visible paper texture and soft washes, warm golden light, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book
for young readers. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers.
