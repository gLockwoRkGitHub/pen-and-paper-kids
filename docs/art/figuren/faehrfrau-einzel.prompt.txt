One old ferrywoman standing alone on plain cream watercolor paper, full body, three-quarter front view, centered, soft ground shadow. A tall, wiry human woman about seventy, very upright, a long white braid down her back, a lean weathered face with sharp dark eyes; a moss-green wool tunic, a brown leather vest, trousers rolled up to the knee, bare feet. She holds a long wooden punting pole; near its top a small sign of four interlinked rings is carved into the wood. Calm, watchful, keeps secrets. Fantasy setting, no modern clothing.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture and soft washes, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book for young readers. Plain background, no scenery. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no signature.
