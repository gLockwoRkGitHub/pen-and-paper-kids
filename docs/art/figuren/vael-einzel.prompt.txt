One gaunt old hermit man standing alone on plain cream watercolor paper, full body, three-quarter front view, centered, soft ground shadow. He is very thin and tall, about eighty, with a long neck, bony hands, wide surprised pale-blue eyes and a shy, gentle smile. His thin white hair is very soft and wispy and stands out in a fluffy round cloud all around his head, like cotton wool. Just hair: no flowers, no seeds, no stems, no hat. He wears a patched, faded sky-grey coat that is too big for him and flutters as if in a breeze, a long thin scarf blowing sideways, worn trousers and simple sandals. Shy and odd but kind. Fantasy lighthouse-keeper setting, no modern clothing, not scary.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture and soft washes, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book for young readers. Plain background, no scenery. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers.
