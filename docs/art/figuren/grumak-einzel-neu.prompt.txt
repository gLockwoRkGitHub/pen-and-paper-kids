# Grumak, neues Einzelbild: groß wie ein Schrank (docs/worldbuilding/figuren-aussehen.md), ohne Requisit
One gigantic, gentle orc spice merchant standing alone on plain cream watercolor paper, full body, three-quarter front view, centered, soft ground shadow. He is built like a wardrobe: towering, far taller and much more massive than any human, with enormously broad square shoulders, a huge barrel chest, a thick neck, long heavy arms as thick as tree trunks and hands like shovels. His head looks small on his giant body, his legs are short and sturdy. Next to him, drawn only as a faint light pencil outline for scale, stands the silhouette of an ordinary adult human who reaches only up to his chest. Grey-green skin, pointed ears with small gold rings in both ears, two large clearly visible lower tusks rising from his lower jaw, a broad kind face with tired, worried eyes and a warm half-smile. Long black hair with clearly visible grey streaks, tied back. He wears a loose rust-red merchant tunic with gold trim, a wide brown leather belt hung with small spice pouches, a saffron-yellow sash, dark baggy trousers and sturdy sandals. Both hands empty, hanging relaxed at his sides. Good-natured, not threatening, not a warrior, no weapons, no armor. Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture and soft washes, warm golden light, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book for young readers. Plain background, no scenery. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers.
