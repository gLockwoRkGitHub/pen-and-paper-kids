# Cassian, Einzelbild (Vorschlag von Claude – gleiche Farben und Kleidung wie Liora, es ist dieselbe Rolle)
One eleven-year-old boy standing alone on plain cream watercolor paper, full body, three-quarter front view,
centered, soft ground shadow. He is the adventurous son of a very rich merchant family
who has just fallen out of an apple tree: messy short chestnut-brown curly hair with a few green
leaves stuck in it, freckles, bright hazel eyes, a broad cheeky grin. He wears an expensive
deep-green velvet jacket with small gold buttons, the left sleeve torn open at the shoulder,
a white linen shirt with a crumpled collar, dark brown knee breeches with grass stains on the knees,
white stockings, and fine brown leather shoes with silver buckles. Hands on his hips, confident and friendly.
Fantasy harbor-town setting, no modern clothing.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines,
visible paper texture and soft washes, warm golden light, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book
for young readers. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers.
