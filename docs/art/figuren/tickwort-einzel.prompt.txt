# Tickwort, Einzelbild (Grundlage für das Referenzblatt) – Zeilen mit # werden ignoriert
One tiny squat steampunk automaton standing alone on plain cream watercolor paper, full body,
three-quarter front view seen slightly from above, centered, soft ground shadow.
The automaton is made of warm polished brass with copper rivets. It is not humanoid.
Its body is one big, perfectly round brass ball like a pot-bellied kettle, with a small hinged hatch door
in the front. The ball stands low to the ground on a tripod of three very short stubby brass peg legs,
all three legs clearly visible and spread apart like the legs of a three-legged cauldron:
one leg in front in the middle, one at the back left, one at the back right. No knees, no feet, no shoes.
On top of the ball sits a much smaller dome-shaped head with one single large round glass eye
like a camera lens with a soft amber glow, no mouth, no ears. A tiny brass chimney on top of the head
puffs a little white steam cloud. Two thin spindly jointed arms ending in simple two-pronged brass pincer claws like small pliers,
no fingers, no human hands.
Friendly, curious, slightly clumsy and lovable, not scary.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines,
visible paper texture and soft washes, warm golden light, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book
for young readers. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers.
