Character turnaround sheet of exactly the same brass automaton as in the reference image,
shown four times side by side in one row on plain cream watercolor paper, all the same size:
front view, three-quarter view, side view facing right, back view.
Keep the design identical in every view: big round brass ball body with the small hinged hatch door
on the belly, small dome-shaped head with one single large round amber glass eye, tiny chimney on the head
puffing a little white steam cloud, two thin spindly jointed arms ending in simple two-pronged
brass pincer claws like small pliers (no fingers, no human hands),
and a tripod of exactly three very short stubby peg legs (one in front, two behind), no feet, not humanoid.
In the back view the eye and the hatch are not visible.
Same art style as the reference: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache
with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture, warm golden light, soft ground shadows.
No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers.
