# Vorschlag von Claude (nicht festgelegt): Mensch um die fünfzig, schütteres graues Haar, schmale Brille.
One thin, bony middle-aged man standing alone on plain cream watercolor paper, full body,
three-quarter front view, centered, soft ground shadow. He is a debt collector for a bank:
tall and gaunt, slightly stooped, with a very long sharp nose that looks as if it could smell coins,
thin grey hair combed back, narrow shrewd eyes, a thin humourless smile. He wears a long, perfectly neat
silver-grey coat buttoned to the neck, with one small shiny silver coin pinned to the collar as a badge,
grey trousers and polished black shoes. He carries a thick leather-bound ledger book under one arm
and holds a quill pen in the other hand. Cold, precise and unpleasant, but not a monster and not violent.
Fantasy harbor-town setting, no modern clothing, no weapons.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines,
visible paper texture and soft washes, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book
for young readers. Plain background, no scenery. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers.
