# Ortsbild Gewölbe unter dem Markt (Perle 1 Erwähnung, Perle 3). 1536x1024.
Old underground vaults beneath a market square, seen from the bottom of a worn stone staircase. Low round stone arches, several dark corridors branching off, niches, old barrels and wine racks, moss in the joints, dripping water and a little gutter in the floor. A shaft of daylight falls down the staircase; the rest is dark. Mysterious but not scary. No people.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture and soft washes, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book for young readers. Full-bleed scene, no border. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no signature.
