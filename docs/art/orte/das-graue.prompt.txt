# Ortsbild Das Graue (Perle 4). 1536x1024.
Seen from a hilltop: a shallow hollow at the foot of the mountains. The foreground is green with yellow gorse. In the hollow, an area about as big as a town square has turned completely grey. Inside the grey area everything is drained of color like a washed-out watercolor: ash-grey grass, grey stones, grey animals; the border to the green is soft but clear. No fog, no smoke, no monsters, no faces in the grey; eerie only through stillness and missing color. A dead grey bush in the middle. Nothing moves. No people, no animals.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture and soft washes, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book for young readers. Full-bleed scene, no border. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no signature.
