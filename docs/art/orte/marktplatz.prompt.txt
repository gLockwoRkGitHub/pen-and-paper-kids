# Ortsbild Marktplatz von Lysmara mit Ratshalle und Brunnen. 1536x1024.
Wide view of the central market square of a fantasy port town at eye level. On the far side the town hall: a stately building of pale stone with a wide flight of steps, columns and a bell tower; above the door a stone relief of a pearl in a scallop shell. In the middle of the square a round stone fountain with a basin. Around the square tall narrow houses with colorful shutters, market stalls with striped awnings and, near the fountain, a large spice stall under a red-and-gold sun sail. A stone archway leads out of the square on the right side. Only a few people, morning light.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture and soft washes, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book for young readers. Full-bleed scene, no border. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no signature.
