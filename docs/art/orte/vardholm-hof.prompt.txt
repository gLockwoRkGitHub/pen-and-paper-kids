# Ortsbild Hof von Vardholm (Perle 6). 1536x1024.
The large stone courtyard inside a mountain fortress, enclosed by high grey walls and the sheer rock faces of a gorge. In the courtyard rows of waist-high round bronze plates on stone pedestals, connected by thick bronze pipes that run across the paving and up the side of the tallest tower to a large round mirror at its top. A wooden grandstand with black and gold banners (balance-scale emblem only, no letters) along one side, stables, a well, torches in iron holders on the walls. Evening light. No people.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture and soft washes, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book for young readers. Full-bleed scene, no border. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no signature.
