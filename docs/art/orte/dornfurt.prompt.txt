# Ortsbild Dornfurt (Perle 7). 1536x1024.
A small walled town on a wide shallow river ford beyond a mountain range, enclosed by a palisade of sharpened wooden stakes. Timber houses with steep roofs, several small bell towers, a market square with a big drum on a wooden stand, a rope ferry (a wide raft on a rope) crossing the river, a long wooden jetty with torches. Outside the palisade, tents and carts of refugees, and a separate small camp by the river. Behind the town brown hills and birch woods; far in the west the huge grey mountain wall. Busy, crowded, lively. No readable signs.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture and soft washes, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book for young readers. Full-bleed scene, no border. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no signature.
