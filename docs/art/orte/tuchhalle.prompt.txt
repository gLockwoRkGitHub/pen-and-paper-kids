# Ortsbild Tuchhalle von Webstuhl und Amboss (Perle 2 und 3). 1536x1024.
Interior of a huge weaving hall as big as a church nave, seen from the entrance at eye level. Hundreds of wooden looms in long rows, driven by belts and wooden shafts from the ceiling; long bolts of dyed cloth hang from the rafters in poppy red, ink blue and mustard yellow. In the middle a small wooden podium. Tall arched windows on one side, through which the paddles of a gigantic water wheel are visible outside. Dust dancing in sunbeams. No people.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture and soft washes, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book for young readers. Full-bleed scene, no border. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no signature.
