# Ortsbild Räderviertel am Orin (Perle 2, Referenz für alle Bilder am Fluss). 1536x1024.
The craftsmen's quarter of a harbor city along a cold, fast mountain river. Along the bank a row of huge wooden water wheels as tall as houses, turning, driving workshops behind them. Stone and timber workshops with smoking chimneys; a dye works releasing colorful steam (red, blue, yellow). In the middle a great smithy with a wide open front and a glowing forge, beside it a narrow tall wooden drying tower with long colored cloth strips hanging from its levels and a small platform at the top, standing directly at a foaming mill race that shoots towards the biggest wheel. Further downstream an old stone bridge over the river and a quay with flat ore barges. In the far east grey mountains with a narrow notch. Busy, noisy, warm. No people.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture and soft washes, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book for young readers. Full-bleed scene, no border. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no signature.
