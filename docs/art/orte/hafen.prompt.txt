# Ortsbild Hafen von Lysmara (ohne Hauptfiguren). 1536x1024.
Wide view of the harbor of a fantasy port town, seen from the quay at eye level. A round harbor bay with old stone quay walls, wooden piers, sailing ships and fishing boats, crates, barrels and coiled ropes. On the quay stands a large brass steam crane with a long wooden boom, a puffing boiler and a cargo net hanging from a rope. At the north side a row of tall old warehouses, at the head of the harbor an old customs house with a big stone archway. Behind the roofs rises the town up a hill. A few dockworkers far away, seagulls. Bright late morning, sea-green water.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture and soft washes, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book for young readers. Full-bleed scene, no border. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no signature.
