# Ortsbild Vardholm von außen (Perle 6). 1536x1024.
A narrow, extremely deep gorge cut into a huge grey mountain wall as if by a giant's axe. Across the whole width of the gorge stands a grey stone fortress: thick walls, three square towers, a gate of black iron. On top of the highest tower a large round mirror of polished bronze and glass on an iron frame, glinting like a giant eye. Bronze pipes run down the outside of the tower into the rock. Snow on the peaks above, a mountain stream at the bottom of the gorge, the gravel road leading up to the gate. Imposing, cold, impressive. No people.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture and soft washes, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book for young readers. Full-bleed scene, no border. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no signature.
