# Ortsbild Herz des Berges (Perle 5). 1536x1024.
# Zeichen des Kreises der Vier = vier verschlungene Ringe (docs/art/requisiten/ringzeichen.png). Prüfen: keine Drehform/kein Windrad, keine Audi-Reihe, keine olympische Anordnung.
An immense dark cavern whose walls are lost in darkness. Inlaid in the polished floor, a gigantic sign made of pale stone: four interlinked rings arranged in a closed circle like a four-leaf clover (each ring linked only with its two neighbours, the centre left open). Above its center floats a crystal sphere as big as a house; inside it four lights slowly circle each other: earthy brown, water blue, fire red and white. A dark jagged crack runs through the crystal; where it is, the lights are pale, and colorless grey sinks from it to the floor and turns the stone lines grey. In the right wall a freshly hacked rough tunnel with pickaxe marks and wooden crates. Awe-inspiring and wounded. No people.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture and soft washes, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book for young readers. Full-bleed scene, no border. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no signature.
