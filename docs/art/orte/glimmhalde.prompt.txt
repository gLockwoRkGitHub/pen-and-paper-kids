# Ortsbild Glimmhalde, Höhlenstadt der Kobolde (Perle 5). 1536x1024.
A gigantic cave whose ceiling disappears in darkness. On the walls small houses cling like swallow nests, connected by ladders, walkways and rope bridges, lit by hundreds of small miner's lamps in yellow, green and blue. Below lies a black mirror-smooth underground lake with rafts carrying gardens of glowing mushrooms. Beautiful and quiet. No people.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture and soft washes, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book for young readers. Full-bleed scene, no border. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no signature.
