# Ortsbild Klippen von Ahrn mit Leuchtturm (Perle 1, Perle 2 Vael). 1536x1024.
Wide view along a high rugged sea cliff coast. Grassy windswept highland with yellow gorse bushes and grey rocks on the right, a steep drop to the left with white surf crashing against dark rocks far below. On the outermost cliff stands an old white lighthouse that leans noticeably to one side like a broken tooth, with a small iron lantern room on top and a heavy wooden door. A narrow footpath leads towards it. Windy day, fast clouds, spray in the air. No people.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture and soft washes, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book for young readers. Full-bleed scene, no border. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no signature.
