# Ortsbild Hof des Zirkels der Glühenden Phiole am Hafen (Perle 3). 1536x1024.
A cobbled courtyard of an alchemists' guild house by the harbor. A tall narrow house with crooked windows and a chimney puffing violet smoke, a red banner with a vial and golden smoke. In the courtyard eight small copper cauldrons on eight small fire pits in two rows, tables with bowls of ingredients, shelves of colorful bottles. A wooden fence on one side, behind it the neighbouring goblin workshop with a crooked chimney. Masts of ships visible above the roofs. Sunny. No people.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture and soft washes, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book for young readers. Full-bleed scene, no border. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no signature.
