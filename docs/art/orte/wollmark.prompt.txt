# Ortsbild Wollmark (Perle 4). 1536x1024.
A shepherds' village in wide green hill country: low houses of fieldstone with turf roofs on which grass grows, big barns with open doors full of wool bales up to the roof, stone-walled sheep pens, a well in the middle of a small square, a dirt road running through. Three black tents with a golden balance-scale emblem stand on the square. Behind the village green hills and far away a high grey mountain range with snowfields. Late afternoon. No people.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture and soft washes, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book for young readers. Full-bleed scene, no border. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no signature.
