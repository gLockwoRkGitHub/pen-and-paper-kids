# Ortsbild Minentor (Perle 5). 1536x1024.
A gigantic rectangular gate cut into a mountain wall, as tall as a town hall, with square-cut angular dwarven carvings and geometric ornaments around it. Rails with ore carts lead out of the gate down to a fast green river. Beside it a wooden guild house with a weaving-shuttle-and-hammer sign and a bench. Evening glow on the rock, warm lamplight from inside the gate. No people.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture and soft washes, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book for young readers. Full-bleed scene, no border. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no signature.
