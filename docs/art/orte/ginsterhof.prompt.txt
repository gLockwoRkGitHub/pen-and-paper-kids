# Ortsbild Ginsterhof (Perle 4). 1536x1024.
A lonely crooked fieldstone farmhouse with a crooked chimney and a goat shed at the foot of steep mountains, a wooden bench by the door, yellow gorse and grey boulders all around, a little brook. Goats everywhere, even on the roof. Behind it the mountainside rises steeply, and high up a thin path zigzags up the slope. Sunny morning. No people.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture and soft washes, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book for young readers. Full-bleed scene, no border. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no signature.
