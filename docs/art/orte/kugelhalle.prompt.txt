# Ortsbild Kugelhalle unter dem Nordturm (Perle 6). 1536x1024.
A long, high hall carved into the rock like a church nave. Floor-to-ceiling shelves, row after row, filled with thousands of small iron baskets; in every basket a glass orb the size of an apple with a flickering light inside: earthy brown, water blue, fire red and white. The orbs fill the hall with a restless, many-colored glow, like a swarm of bees in a jar. At the far end thick bronze pipes rise into the ceiling. Beautiful and unsettling. No people.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture and soft washes, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book for young readers. Full-bleed scene, no border. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no signature.
