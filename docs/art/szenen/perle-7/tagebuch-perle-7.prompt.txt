# 23 · tagebuch/perle-7 · Ebene Tagebuch · Referenzen: karten/meridel-lagevorlage.png · 1536x1024
An open handmade travel journal on a rough inn table by candlelight, seen from above. Across both cream pages a complete charcoal and watercolor map of a land enclosed by a horseshoe of mountains, open to the sea in the west: a harbor city, green meadows, a river, mines in the northeast, a fortress in a gorge in the east. For the first time the drawing goes beyond the mountains on the right page: a river, a small town with a palisade, and a bold arrow pointing east off the page. In the corner a small spiral of four lines. Beside the book a brass compass, a gold button and a coiled rope. Lines of handwriting only as illegible squiggles.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture and soft washes, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book for young readers. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no signature.
