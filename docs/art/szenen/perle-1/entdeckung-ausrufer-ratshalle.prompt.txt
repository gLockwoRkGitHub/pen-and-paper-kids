From inside a crowd on the market square, looking up the wide stone steps of a stately pale town hall with columns and a bell tower topped by a simple weather vane shaped like a fish (no cross, no religious symbols); pigeons burst up from the tower. On the top step a gaunt herald in a long bright red coat and a black beret with a white feather unrolls a parchment and proclaims. Around him in a half circle stand six to eight mercenaries in long black coats with grey steel breastplates each showing a clearly visible golden balance-scale emblem, motionless like dark silhouettes. In the foreground the backs and heads of the crowd of many peoples: humans, dwarves, small gnomes, one huge orc; raised fists, worried faces in profile. At the far right edge, small, in the shadow under a stone archway, a tall grey hooded figure; on the pillar of that archway a small sign of four interlinked rings arranged in a closed circle like a four-leaf clover (each ring linked only with its two neighbours, the centre left open) is drawn in chalk. Late afternoon, slanting golden light. A door to the outside world is slamming shut.
