View from a high rooftop at the top of the town, looking south over a fantasy port town at golden evening. On the right (west) the open sea with a low glowing sun above the water; in front of it a round harbor bay with ship masts and a brass steam crane. In the middle a sea of red and brown roofs, chimneys with thin smoke, a pale stone town hall with a bell tower. On the left side a river with huge wooden water wheels at the edge of the town, beyond it wide green pastures, and far behind them a long grey wall of high mountains with snowy peaks that has one single narrow notch cut into it. The town lies between mountains and sea like a pearl in a shell. Warm lit windows, the mountains cool blue-grey, sense of a big adventure waiting. On the far left horizon one continuous straight grey mountain wall like a fortress wall, all the same height, with a single deep narrow V-shaped notch cut into its middle; no separate peaks.
First-person point of view at the eye height of a twelve-year-old child. The viewer is not visible: no hands, no arms, no legs, no feet, no body parts, no shadow and no reflection of the viewer.
The painted scene fills the center; towards the edges it gradually dissolves into loose pencil sketch lines, unfinished ink hatching and splashes of watercolor on cream paper.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture and soft washes, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book for young readers. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no signature.
