# Die Magie geht vom Betrachter aus, ohne Hände. Schutz, kein Angriff.
Night on a mountain slope, looking uphill towards a colorless grey veil that creeps down from the mountains. Dew, brook water and mist rise from the meadow directly in front of the viewer into a glittering silver veil that spreads between the viewer and the grey; where the grey touches it, it drips and shimmers. Silver light. In the background gnolls throw their heads back and howl, and sleepwalking figures stop and blink. The grey retreats, and at its edge color returns to the grass. Triumphant, awe-inspiring, protective.
First-person point of view at the eye height of a twelve-year-old child. The viewer is not visible: no hands, no arms, no legs, no feet, no body parts, no shadow and no reflection of the viewer.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture and soft washes, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book for young readers. Full-bleed scene, no border. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no signature.
