# Wichtigstes neues Bild: unheimlich durch Stille und fehlende Farbe, nicht durch etwas, das angreift.
From a hilltop looking down into a hollow at the foot of the mountains. The foreground is green with yellow gorse. Beyond a soft line, an area as big as a town square is completely grey. Inside the grey area everything is drained of color like a washed-out watercolor: ash-grey grass, grey stones, grey animals; the border to the green is soft but clear. No fog, no smoke, no monsters, no faces in the grey; eerie only through stillness and missing color. At the edge of the grey stand a dozen sheep, all facing its center, motionless, half-grey themselves. Further inside the grey stands a small gnoll cub with big round ears, equally motionless, eyes open. On the left edge an old goatherd with a pipe has stopped and stretches out his arm as if to hold the viewer back. Mountains above, a pale sky. Absolute stillness.
First-person point of view at the eye height of a twelve-year-old child. The viewer is not visible: no hands, no arms, no legs, no feet, no body parts, no shadow and no reflection of the viewer.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture and soft washes, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book for young readers. Full-bleed scene, no border. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no signature.
