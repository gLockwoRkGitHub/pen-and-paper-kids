# Die Magie geht vom Betrachter aus, ohne Hände. Schutz, kein Angriff. Größer als die Rufe in Perle 4.
Night in the fortress courtyard, looking east up the gorge. Every torch on the walls flares up at once, and before the viewer a towering column of roaring orange flame rises as high as the tower, throwing sparks into the sky. Around the viewer dozens of people with brass plaques raise their hands, each with a small colored light. The ash-grey colorless band in the gorge is pushed back, and where it retreats color returns. In the background the great mirror on the tower has a crack from top to bottom. The grey is not fog and not smoke: it is simply color draining away, everything it touches becomes desaturated ash-grey like a washed-out watercolor, with a soft but clear border; no monsters, no faces in the grey. Triumphant, awe-inspiring, many voices together.
First-person point of view at the eye height of a twelve-year-old child. The viewer is not visible: no hands, no arms, no legs, no feet, no body parts, no shadow and no reflection of the viewer.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture and soft washes, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book for young readers.
