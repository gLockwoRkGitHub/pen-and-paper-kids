Bright morning in the fortress courtyard, sun rising over the pass at the end of the gorge. The great mirror on the tower has a crack from top to bottom. Registered mages cook soup over small open fires, steam rises, someone laughs; mercenaries sit tired against the wall with their helmets off. Far away at the end of the gorge only a faint grey haze lies on the rocks like dust on a shelf. Relief after a long night, warm colors returning.
