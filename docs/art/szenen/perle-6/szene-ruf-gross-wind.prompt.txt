# Die Magie geht vom Betrachter aus, ohne Hände. Schutz, kein Angriff. Größer als die Rufe in Perle 4.
Night in the fortress courtyard, looking east up the gorge. A howling white storm sweeps down from the snowy peaks through the gorge, banners crack, shutters slam, swirls of white wind are drawn as bright curving lines. Around the viewer dozens of people with brass plaques raise their hands, each with a small colored light. The ash-grey colorless band in the gorge is blown back, and where it retreats the rocks and grass regain their color. In the background the great mirror on the tower has a crack from top to bottom. The grey is not fog and not smoke: it is simply color draining away, everything it touches becomes desaturated ash-grey like a washed-out watercolor, with a soft but clear border; no monsters, no faces in the grey. Triumphant, awe-inspiring, many voices together.
