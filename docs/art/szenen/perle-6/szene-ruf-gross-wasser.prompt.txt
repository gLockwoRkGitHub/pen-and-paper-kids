Night in the fortress courtyard, looking east up the gorge. The mountain stream rears up into a roaring wave of bright blue water and foam that rushes up the gorge against the ash-grey colorless band, pushing it back; spray glitters in torchlight. Around the viewer dozens of people with brass plaques raise their hands, each with a small colored light. Where the grey retreats the rocks and grass regain their color. In the background the great mirror on the tower has a crack from top to bottom. The grey is not fog and not smoke: it is simply color draining away, everything it touches becomes desaturated ash-grey like a washed-out watercolor, with a soft but clear border; no monsters, no faces in the grey. Triumphant, awe-inspiring, many voices together. Foreground: a crowd of people of all ages raising small colored lights; the blue wave races away from the viewer up the gorge into an ash-grey colorless area, which turns back to color where the water passes.
First-person point of view at the eye height of a twelve-year-old child. The viewer is not visible: no hands, no arms, no legs, no feet, no body parts, no shadow and no reflection of the viewer.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture and soft washes, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book for young readers. Full-bleed scene, no border. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no signature.
