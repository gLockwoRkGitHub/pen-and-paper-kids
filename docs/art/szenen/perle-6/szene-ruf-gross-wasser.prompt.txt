# Die Magie geht vom Betrachter aus, ohne Hände. Schutz, kein Angriff. Größer als die Rufe in Perle 4.
Night in the fortress courtyard, looking east up the gorge. The mountain stream rears up into a roaring wave of bright blue water and foam that rushes up the gorge against the ash-grey colorless band, pushing it back; spray glitters in torchlight. Around the viewer dozens of people with brass plaques raise their hands, each with a small colored light. Where the grey retreats the rocks and grass regain their color. In the background the great mirror on the tower has a crack from top to bottom. The grey is not fog and not smoke: it is simply color draining away, everything it touches becomes desaturated ash-grey like a washed-out watercolor, with a soft but clear border; no monsters, no faces in the grey. Triumphant, awe-inspiring, many voices together.
