# Die Magie geht vom Betrachter aus, ohne Hände.
# 20.10.2026 (Bildprüfung 19.10.2026): Prompt-Vorschlag aus docs/art/bildpruefung-2026-10-19.md ergänzt.
The flames on a burning wooden drying tower have stopped as if someone called their name, and flow down the tower like glowing honey, across the paving stones towards the viewer, gathering in the air right in front of the viewer into a small, warm, trembling ball of orange light. The tower smokes but no longer burns. On the platform at the top, small, a twelve-year-old boy with short unruly chestnut curls escaping from under a flat cap, freckles, hazel eyes, wearing a far too big brown worker's jacket with rolled-up sleeves over a dark-green velvet jacket with small gold buttons that peeks out, and fine leather shoes with silver buckles stares down in amazement. Workers, smiths and kobolds stare. The first awakening of magic: overwhelming, beautiful, a little frightening. The boy stands small on the railed top platform of the drying tower; all workers have normal human faces, and the bottom right corner shows only riverbank.
First-person point of view at the eye height of a twelve-year-old child. The viewer is not visible: no hands, no arms, no legs, no feet, no body parts, no shadow and no reflection of the viewer.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture and soft washes, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book for young readers. Full-bleed scene, no border. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no signature.
