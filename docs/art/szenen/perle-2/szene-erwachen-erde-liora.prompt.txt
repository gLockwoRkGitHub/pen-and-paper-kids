The paving stones beside a burning wooden drying tower burst open, and a staircase of rough rock grows up out of the ground, step upon step, up to the platform at the top; at the foot of the tower the earth heaves like a wave and buries the burning coals. Brown light, flying dust and pebbles. Climbing down the new stone stairs, sooty and trembling, a twelve-year-old girl with chestnut curls (tied back with a dark-green ribbon) escaping from under a flat cap, freckles, hazel eyes, wearing a far too big brown worker's jacket with rolled-up sleeves over a dark-green velvet jacket with small gold buttons that peeks out, and fine leather shoes with silver buckles. Workers, smiths and kobolds stare. The first awakening of magic: overwhelming, beautiful, a little frightening. A rough stone staircase spirals up the side of the smoking wooden tower to its top platform, and the girl is on the upper steps coming down; the fire at the foot is buried under heaped earth, and no one stands in or near any flames.
