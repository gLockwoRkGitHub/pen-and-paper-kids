# Messingplakette der Eingeschriebenen (Perle 3–6). 1024x1024.
One small square brass plaque on a plain cream watercolor paper background, centered, soft shadow, hanging from a simple string. Engraved on it a balance scale with two hanging pans and a short row of small dots instead of a number. Slightly worn, a little scratched.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture and soft washes, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book for young readers. Plain background, no scenery. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no signature.
