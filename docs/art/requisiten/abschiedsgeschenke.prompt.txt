# Abschiedsgeschenke (Perle 7). 1536x1024. Vier Gegenstände nebeneinander, zur Wiedererkennung in Szenen.
Four small objects lying side by side on plain cream watercolor paper, evenly spaced, soft shadows: a neatly coiled hemp rope; a small brass whistle with copper rivets in steampunk style; a heavy plain iron ring with a rough hammered surface; a small round gold button with fine embroidery-like engraving.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture and soft washes, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book for young readers. Plain background, no scenery. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no signature.
