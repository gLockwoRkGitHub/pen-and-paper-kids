# Erzmarke von Grisk (Perle 1). 1024x1024.
One small token made of dark iron ore lying on plain cream watercolor paper, centered, soft shadow. Roughly round, as big as a large coin, heavy-looking, dark grey-black with tiny glittering specks, a hole for a leather cord, and one simple kobold mark scratched into it: three short lines like a pickaxe track. A faint shimmer as if it hums quietly.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture and soft washes, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book for young readers. Plain background, no scenery. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no signature.
