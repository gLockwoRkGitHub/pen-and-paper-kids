# Glaskugel mit gefangenem Blitz (Perle 1 Nordkai). 1024x1024.
One glass orb the size of an apple lying on plain cream watercolor paper, centered, soft shadow. Inside the clear glass a small bright white-blue lightning bolt flickers and branches, trapped, lighting the glass from within; a few tiny sparks cling to the inner surface. A thin brass ring around the middle of the orb. Magical and a little dangerous.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture and soft washes, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book for young readers. Plain background, no scenery. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no signature.
