# Dampfkarren des Gnomen-Obsthändlers (Perle 1). 1216x832.
One small gnome steam cart on plain cream watercolor paper, three-quarter view, centered, soft ground shadow. A wooden fruit cart on three wheels piled high with shiny red apples, with a small brass boiler at the back, a short chimney puffing steam, copper pipes, a bright red valve handle and a brake lever. Neat gnome craftsmanship.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture and soft washes, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book for young readers. Plain background, no scenery. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no signature.
