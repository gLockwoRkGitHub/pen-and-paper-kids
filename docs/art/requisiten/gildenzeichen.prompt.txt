# Gildenzeichen und Plakette (Perle 3). 1536x1024. Ein Blatt mit fünf Gegenständen nebeneinander.
Five small objects lying side by side in one row on plain cream watercolor paper, evenly spaced, soft shadows, seen from above: 1) a small enamel badge in deep blue showing a weaving shuttle crossed with a hammer; 2) a small silver brooch shaped like a quill feather; 3) a tiny glass vial with red liquid and a golden stopper on a thin leather cord; 4) a small square brass plaque with a hole for a cord and an engraved balance scale, plain without numbers; 5) a small round flask with a glowing green potion.
Art style: hand-painted storybook illustration, warm watercolor and gouache with confident black ink outlines, visible paper texture and soft washes, like a high-quality illustrated adventure book for young readers. Plain background, no scenery. No text, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no signature.
