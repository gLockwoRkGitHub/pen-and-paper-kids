A hand-drawn overview map of a land, seen from straight above. Along the whole left edge the sea with small wave strokes and a wavy coastline. A horseshoe of jagged mountain peaks runs along the top edge, down the whole right side and along the bottom edge; on the right side, exactly in the middle, one single narrow notch cuts through the mountains. A river starts in the mountains at the upper right, curves in a long gentle arc down to the left and flows into the sea at the middle of the left coast. Beyond the mountains, in a narrow strip along the far right edge, brown hills and a second river running from top to bottom. The wide land inside the horseshoe is empty except for a few very sparse light grass strokes. No towns, no houses, no buildings, no roads, no paths, no bridges, no people, no animals.
